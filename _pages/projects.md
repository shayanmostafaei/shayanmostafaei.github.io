## Multi-Omics Prediction of Dementia and Biological Aging

Integration of genetic, proteomic, metabolomic, clinical, and biological-aging information to improve prediction and characterization of Alzheimer's disease and related dementias.

**Methods:** machine learning, survival modelling, statistical genetics, multi-omics integration, biological aging measures.

---

## Biological Aging and Mortality Prediction

Evaluation of biological aging measures for all-cause mortality prediction and assessment of their prognostic information beyond chronological age across population-based cohorts.

**Methods:** survival analysis, prediction modelling, discrimination analysis, external validation, biological aging measures.

---

## Deep-Learning Polygenic Risk Prediction for ADRD

Development and evaluation of machine-learning approaches for polygenic prediction of Alzheimer's disease and related dementias, including age- and APOE-stratified analyses.

**Methods:** statistical genetics, polygenic risk scores, machine learning, deep learning, risk prediction.

---

## Proteomic and Metabolomic Aging

Development and evaluation of proteomic and metabolomic aging signatures and investigation of their associations with age-related disease and mortality.

**Methods:** proteomics, metabolomics, ensemble learning, biological aging clocks, survival analysis.

---

## Statistical and Machine-Learning Methods in Aging Research

Development and application of reproducible statistical and computational workflows for high-dimensional biomedical and epidemiological data.
